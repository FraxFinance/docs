### Hop Contracts

FraxNet routes cross-chain mints and redemptions through [HopV2](/protocol/crosschain/addresses#hopv2-contracts). The `FraxNetDepositFactory` on Ethereum is configured with the Ethereum Hop, which relays through the Fraxtal Hop to the destination chain.

| Chain    | `Hop`                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ethereum | [`0x0000006D38568b00B457580b734e0076C62de659`](https://etherscan.io/address/0x0000006D38568b00B457580b734e0076C62de659) |
| Fraxtal  | [`0x00000000e18aFc20Afe54d4B2C8688bB60c06B36`](https://fraxscan.com/address/0x00000000e18aFc20Afe54d4B2C8688bB60c06B36) |

Every other chain in the mesh uses the Hop at `0x0000006D38568b00B457580b734e0076C62de659`. See [HopV2 Contracts](/protocol/crosschain/addresses#hopv2-contracts) for the full list.
