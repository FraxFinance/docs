# FraxZero Supported Networks and EIDs

### FraxZero Hop Supported Networks

frxUSD cross-chain transfers are available between the following networks using the Hop method (HopV2). A transfer between two networks other than Fraxtal is relayed through Fraxtal automatically and is submitted as a single transaction on the source network.

The Hop address is `0x0000006D38568b00B457580b734e0076C62de659` on every network except Fraxtal, where it is `0x00000000e18aFc20Afe54d4B2C8688bB60c06B36`.

**Note:** If Ethereum mainnet is the source chain using the Hop method, you must use the [Ethereum Lockbox address](/frxusd/fraxzero-supported-networks#fraxzero-lockbox-supported-networks) for `quote` and `sendOFT` methods.

**Note:** The Hop on Tempo does not accept `msg.value` and collects its fee in a Tempo stablecoin instead. See [Tempo](/protocol/crosschain/tempo) before sending from Tempo.

Follow our [Transfer frxUSD from EVM chains](/frxusd/cross-chain-transfers-quickstarts/transfer-from-evm) guide to see it in action.

HopV2 keeps the same `sendOFT(address,uint32,bytes32,uint256)` function, but its `quote` function takes two additional arguments (`dstGas` and `data`) and returns the fee as a `uint256`.

### FraxZero Solana

frxUSD cross-chain transfers between Solana and Fraxtal are supported.

Follow our [Transfer frxUSD from Solana](/frxusd/cross-chain-transfers-quickstarts/transfer-from-solana) guide to get started.

**Note:** frxUSD on Solana only has **9 decimals** vs the typical 18 decimals on EVM chains.
