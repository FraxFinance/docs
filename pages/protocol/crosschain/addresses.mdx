## HopV2 Contracts

All cross-chain routes relay through the Fraxtal hub. Fraxtal runs `FraxtalHopV2`, and each other chain runs a `RemoteHopV2` and a `RemoteAdmin` contract (for Fraxtal-initiated admin operations). Every Hop runs version `2.0.1`, and Tempo runs the `RemoteHopV201Tempo` variant (see [Tempo](./tempo)). Before routing to a chain, check that it is registered on the Fraxtal Hop with `remoteHop(dstEid)`.
